            From rover to outpost

            The fictional crew scenario combines two separate real events. Energy, reserves, crop progress and radiation scores are simplified training units.

            2018 · Opportunity dust storm

            Opportunity had 21 Wh available on sol 5111. Six points derived from the chart anchor the tightening of the power budget. Intermediate and recovery turns are modeled. Sol labels indicate selected dates in the scenario, and turns compress time. Original NASA chart ↗

            The outpost sensor shows a simulated opacity index.

            2024 · Curiosity watches a solar storm

            NASA reported a dose of 8,100 μGy for the May 20 event at Curiosity's location. In the game, the shelter walls reduce exposure. Ventilation draws power to keep air safe inside. Curiosity RAD event ↗

            Technical sources for the simulation

            • NASA NTRS · Mars Surface Power Generation — dust opacity explains the drop in available solar energy.
            • NASA NTRS · Veggie technical paper — light, water and delayed growth. The ISS lettuce experiment took 33 days.
            • NASA NTRS · Perseverance presentation — samples are collected from different targets with context. The lab and its results in this mission are fictional.
