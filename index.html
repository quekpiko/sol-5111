MISSION DEBRIEF

Why can switching off a greenhouse help the crew?

Grow lights and life support share the same power supply. Dimming the lights can preserve battery charge for the crew, but keeping them off for too long can delay the harvest until the food supply runs out.

SCIENCE NOTES

From rover to outpost

The fictional crew scenario combines two separate real events. Energy, reserves, crop progress and radiation scores are simplified training units.

2018 · Opportunity dust storm

Opportunity had 21 Wh available on sol 5111. Five approximate readings of the descending energy curve plus the published endpoint set the six solar budgets (10, 10, 7, 6, 2, 1). The turns sample different sols and compress time. Original NASA chart ↗

2024 · Curiosity watches a solar storm

NASA reported a dose of 8,100 μGy for the May 20 event at Curiosity's location. This observation inspires the final shelter decision. The game assigns 1 or 5 exposure points for teaching purposes. Curiosity RAD event ↗
